## Our pledge

KeywordNotify is part of the RokNut community. We want everyone who reports a bug, suggests an idea, improves the docs or writes code to feel welcome and respected, whatever their age, background, experience level, identity or opinions.

## What we expect

- **Be respectful.** Treat others the way you'd like to be treated.
- **Be patient.** Many of us are learning. Questions are welcome.
- **Be constructive.** Critique the work, not the person. Explain *why* and suggest a better way.
- **Be honest.** Say how your code was made, including any AI help, and admit mistakes.
- **Assume good intent,** and ask for clarification before assuming the worst.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't share other people's personal information.

## What we don't tolerate

- Harassment, bullying, threats or personal attacks
- Hate speech, slurs, or discrimination of any kind
- Sexual content or unwelcome sexual attention
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission
- Pressuring people to break the plugin rules (selfbots, API spam and similar)
- Anything else that would be unacceptable in a professional, friendly community

## Where this applies

This Code of Conduct applies to this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions and comments) and to official RokNut Academy spaces, including our Discord server, when you're representing or discussing this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, lock discussions, and temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior breaks this Code of Conduct. We'll try to be fair and to explain our decisions, but we have the final say.

## Reporting a problem

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this Code of Conduct, tell us:

- Message a moderator or admin in the [RokNut Academy Discord](https://discord.gg/TNB7rG7EGv), or
- Contact the maintainers through the repository.

Please include what happened, where, and any links or screenshots. Reports are handled with discretion, and we won't tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.
